RECORD YOUR OWN GAME SOUNDS!
============================

Record a sound (Voice Memos on iPhone/iPad works great), name the file
after one of the sounds below, and drop it in this folder. The game will
use YOUR recording instead of the built-in beep. Formats: .m4a .mp3 .wav .ogg

  hungry.m4a     - when a character gets hungry ("I'm hungryyyy!")
  sleepy.m4a     - when a character gets sleepy (a big yawn!)
  crash.m4a      - when a pet breaks something ("uh oh!")
  sparkle.m4a    - when a mess gets fixed ("ta-da!")
  transform.m4a  - the transformation machine ("bzzzzap!")
  boing.m4a      - cartwheel ("wheee!")
  door.m4a       - the front door
  coin.m4a       - earning a coin
  chaching.m4a   - buying clothes
  deny.m4a       - not enough coins ("aww")
  gift.m4a       - a gift appears ("a present!")
  munch.m4a      - eating sounds
  meow.m4a       - petting the cat
  woof.m4a       - petting the dog
  pop.m4a        - tapping the floor
  whoosh.m4a     - entering the transformation machine
  hug.m4a        - characters hugging ("awww!")
  gulp.m4a       - lizard eating a friend ("GULP!")
  water.m4a      - the shower running
  sizzle.m4a     - cooking on the stove

Tip: keep recordings short (under 2 seconds) - they play a lot!
Reload the game page after adding files.
